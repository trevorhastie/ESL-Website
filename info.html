Information for Course Instructors


We have made available for academic course instructors a copy of all the figures in Elements of Statistical Learning (second edition) .

For each of the 18 chapters we have created postscript and pdf files, with one slide per figure in an enlarged format. The captions are included. Here is a sample from chapter 2. To access all the figures, proceed to the figures directory . You will find

  • a gzipped package allps.tgz consisting of all the postscript files (one per chapter),
  • a gzipped package allpdf.tgz with all the files in pdf format,
  • separate postscript and pdf files for each chapter.

The authors retain the copyright for all these figures. They can be used in academic presentations. They cannot be used in publications without the consent of the authors.

We realize that some of these figures are not perfect. In particular, some are too big, and the caption does not fit in to the page. If this causes headaches for users, let us know and we will do some more work to clean them up further.

Comments and criticisms are welcome.
